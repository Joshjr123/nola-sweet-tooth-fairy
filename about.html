Our story

From Kitchen Dream to Sweet Success: Meet the Owner

My name is Shakairione, and I am the proud founder of NOLA Sweet Tooth Fairy.

Long before I owned a business, my kitchen was filled with the aroma of freshly baked desserts. What began as a love for baking during family gatherings and late nights in the kitchen gradually grew into a dream of sharing my creations with others.

As a Black woman entrepreneur, the journey came with challenges and moments of uncertainty. Yet those experiences taught me that your circumstances do not define your future, and purpose is stronger than self-doubt.

Baking and cooking have been part of my life since childhood. Over time, I realized desserts are about more than satisfying a sweet craving — they help create meaningful memories. Whether it's a birthday, wedding, baby shower, family gathering, or a simple treat after a long day, each order allows me to be part of someone's special moment.

At NOLA Sweet Tooth Fairy, every dessert is made with care, intention, and love. My goal is to provide delicious treats while creating an experience that leaves people smiling long after the last bite.

To anyone pursuing a dream, I hope my story encourages you to keep going. Success takes courage, consistency, and faith, even when the path isn't clear.

Thank you for supporting my business, my vision, and my journey. Every order, referral, and customer means more than you know.

With gratitude,

Shakairione Johnson
Founder & Owner
